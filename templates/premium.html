🪷 Why some things are free

I value generosity (dāna). The Buddha's teachings were freely given, so the Theravada Dhamma teachings and the Dhamma articles are free for everyone — no account, no subscription, no level required. The teaching itself is never behind a paywall.

{# A monk owes no tier, so naming the paid plans here would contradict the rest of the page. The AI is the only limit that still applies to them. #} {% if monk_mode %}

With Monk Mode on, that generosity extends to the whole site: every section is free of charge to you, with no plan to buy. The one exception is the AI features, where every message costs real money to run — so those stay limited. The teachings stay open; the AI tools are what keep the project running.

{% else %}

That extends to asking about it: the Dhamma Q&A is free as well, because charging for the follow-up question would be selling the teaching. It shares the same daily message allowance as the AI Tutor — every message costs real money to run, so there is a limit, but the limit is that cost, not a toll on the Dhamma. What you pay for is the tooling built on top: the Thai language course (Thai Reader) and the wider AI features — conversation practice, the exercise generator and Culture Q&A (Thai Master). The teachings stay open; the study tools are what keep the project running.

🪷 Our Mission

ThaiBridge AI builds a bridge between English and Thai speakers — not just across the words, but across the understanding underneath them.

Thai can't be learned stripped of its culture. How polite you are literally changes which words you use, and a huge amount of everyday speech is rooted in Buddhism. So we teach all of it together: the script, the tones, the register, and the context that makes the words land.
