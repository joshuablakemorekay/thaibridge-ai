Chants for each day of the week The chant for the day you were born, and a simple daily practice

In Thai tradition each day of the week has its own number, the power of the day (กำลังวัน, kamlang wan), and its own protection chant. The chant of the day you were born on is recited that number of times. The numbers come from Thai astrology and are a custom, not a requirement of the Buddha's teaching. Every chant below is in this book: each is a protection chant or its opening verse.
