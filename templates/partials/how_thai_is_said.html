🗣️ How Thai people really say it

  • มั๊ย (mái): the question word is written ไหม, but Thai people say it shorter and higher, like มั๊ย, so that is how this app writes it. You will still see ไหม in books and on signs.
  • รึ (rʉ́): หรือ ("or"), as in "are you going or not?", is shortened to รึ in everyday speech, or cut out completely: ไปมั๊ย, "going?"
  • Saying "I": ดิฉัน, the women's word, is not wrong, but it is too formal and hardly used. Thai people use their nickname instead, or a word that shows the age difference: พี่ (pîi) if you are a bit older than the listener, น้อง (nɔ́ɔŋ) if you are younger, or ป้า (bpâa, auntie) or ลุง (luŋ, uncle) if you are a lot older.
  • Your nickname: it can be any name, it does not have to be a Thai one. Or just leave "I" out: จะทำทีหลังนะคะ / นะครับ (jà tam tii-lǎng ná ká / ná kráp), "will do it later." Thai people understand who is speaking.
