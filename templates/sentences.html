🎚️ Formal, Neutral, Casual — what's the difference?

Every main line on this page is formal: full pronoun, ครับ or ค่ะ on the end. That is the right thing to learn first, but it is not how most Thai is actually spoken. So underneath each line you'll now find the same thing said one or two rungs down.

Formal ผมขอข้าวผัดหนึ่งจานครับ{{ listen('ผมขอข้าวผัดหนึ่งจานครับ') }} pǒm kɔ̌ɔ kâao pàt nʉ̀ŋ jaan kráp
Pronoun and particle both present. Strangers, staff, anyone senior to you.
Neutral ขอข้าวผัดจานนึงครับ{{ listen('ขอข้าวผัดจานนึงครับ') }} kɔ̌ɔ kâao pàt jaan nʉ̀ŋ kráp
Pronoun dropped, ครับ kept. Safe with absolutely anyone — this is where most real speech lives.
Casual ข้าวผัดจานนึง{{ listen('ข้าวผัดจานนึง') }} kâao pàt jaan nʉ̀ŋ
Particle gone too. Friends your own age, siblings, a stall you eat at every day.

The test is simply: is ครับ/ค่ะ still there? If yes, you're on neutral. If no, you're on casual. That one question tells you which rung you're standing on every time.

Casual isn't rude — rude is a further step again (กู, มึง), which this app doesn't teach. But casual only ever travels sideways or downwards: to friends, to people younger than you, to children. It never goes upward. Dropping the particle with a stranger, with someone serving you, or with anyone older is where it stops being relaxed and starts being an insult.

Monastic (ภาษาพระสงฆ์) is not a rung on this ladder at all — it swaps the vocabulary rather than the ending (ถวาย not ให้, นิมนต์ not ชวน, อาตมา and โยม for the pronouns). So the two dialogues with a monk in them show a monastic note instead of a casual form, because there genuinely isn't one. This is about politeness, not about region: everything here is Central Thai, and you can read more on the Formality page.

🎯 Practice Suggestions

▼

📝 Writing Practice

  • Write 3 sentences with กำลัง (progressive)
  • Write 3 sentences with แล้ว (completed)
  • Write 3 sentences with จะ (future)
  • Combine them in a short paragraph!

🗣️ Speaking Practice

  • Read conversations aloud
  • Record yourself speaking
  • Practice with language exchange partners
  • Use aspect markers in daily practice

🔄 Transformation Practice

  • Take a present tense sentence
  • Add กำลัง to make it progressive
  • Add แล้ว to make it completed
  • Add จะ to make it future
