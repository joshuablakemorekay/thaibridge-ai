🪷 About ThaiBridge AI เกี่ยวกับ

ThaiBridge AI teaches two related subjects side by side: Theravada Buddhism and the Thai language. The Buddhist side — teachings, suttas, meditation, Dhamma talks and the Digital Chanting Book — is free and open to anyone, in English, with no Thai required. The language side teaches the script, tones, Paiboon romanization, grammar, vocabulary and conversation, supported by an AI tutor. Thai culture and temple etiquette sit in their own section, as context rather than a requirement.

Buddhism is universal; Thai Buddhism is one culture's way of preserving and practising it. Keeping that distinction clear matters to us — we explain it in full here.

This is an honest learning project and portfolio piece, built largely with AI-assisted development. It runs locally and is still being improved — it is not a finished product.
