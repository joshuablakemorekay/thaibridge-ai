What Makes This Different

Reviewed by native speakers

Every lesson has been checked by Kru Mon, a Thai teacher (MA in Education, MSc from Roehampton University), and Phramaha Wichet Suvaddano Sriprom, a Thai monk (MA and PhD from NIDA, Bangkok). Meet them.

Tap to hear any phrase

Thai audio on every lesson phrase, so you hear the tones as well as read them.

JaiScript

Based on Paiboon romanization, which accurately represents Thai pronunciation for English speakers.

A tutor that checks before it answers

The AI tutor looks words up in our confirmed spellings instead of guessing. It also knows the monastic, chanting and cultural material in the course. So its answers connect to Thai way of life and practice rather than being generic. If you'd rather keep that out, choose the culturally neutral option. This takes our understanding to the next level. Thai language, behaviour (etiquette), and culture amongst other factors are so historically influenced and formed based from Buddhist-rich roots. We don't only learn how these roots are so deeply interwoven into the fabrics of Thai society. We learn more about how to be good Buddhists in our own daily lives and practice thanks to the astonishing and unique example the Thais set through their unwavering commitment to the triple gem. Thailand, in my opinion, is a country which undoubtedly sets the most astounding examples of how a whole people can come together in Theravada Buddhism and have it truly emanate the land and all people who enter it. You've never felt such a warm loving-kindness until you've experienced life amongst the Thais in their own country. Thais make a show of these big rich and powerful countries and miserable peoples when it comes to preserving the qualities embodying The Buddha's Dhamma. It shows through their superior level of respect for one another. Their mannerisms. Their kindness. Their willingness to cooperate rather than compete. Their selfless, unegotistical and humble nature which doesn't envy others nor does it desire greedily or with jealousy to own and control everything. It is simply a very happy place.
