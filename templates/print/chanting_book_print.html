About this edition

This is a study edition of the chanting book of Wat Mahathat United Kingdom. The Pali and Thai of every chant are transcribed from the printed book, in the book's order. Around each chant, this edition adds romanised Pali, the ThaiBridge Spelling of the Thai (based on Paiboon+), an English translation, and a short commentary on where the chant comes from, when it is chanted and what it means.

The edition is in three volumes, divided where the printed book divides. Volume 1 holds book pages 1–76: the morning and evening services and the chants appointed for the morning. Volume 2 holds pages 77–190, the chants appointed for the evening. Volume 3 opens at page 191, where the book sets out the services again with a Thai translation, and goes on to its miscellaneous section and appendix. This is Volume {{ volume.number }}.

A note on the English. Most chants are printed in the book in Pali only. Where a chant's English title carries a dagger (†), its English is a working translation prepared for this edition. It is not the book's own and has not yet been checked by a teacher.
