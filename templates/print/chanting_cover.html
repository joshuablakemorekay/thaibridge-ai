Every chant, open to every reader

All 305 chants of the Wat Mahathat United Kingdom chanting book, set out for people who do not read Thai. Each verse is given in five layers:

  • the Pali, in Thai script, as it is chanted
  • the same Pali in the Latin alphabet
  • the Thai translation
  • the Thai in ThaiBridge Spelling, to read it aloud
  • an English translation

Every chant also carries a short note on where it comes from, when it is chanted, and what it means.
