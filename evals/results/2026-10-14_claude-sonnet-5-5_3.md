### `translate-give-to-monk`

**Q:** Translate: I would like to offer this food to the monk.

นิมนต์ฉันอาหารนี้ด้วยครับ/ค่ะ

Actually, the clean version for offering is:

**ผม/ดิฉันขอถวายอาหารนี้แด่พระครับ/ค่ะ**

I'll give the everyday version:

**ขอถวายอาหารนี้ครับ/ค่ะ**
(kɔ̌ɔ tà-wǎai aa-hǎan níi kráp/kâ)
= "I would like to offer this food (to you, Venerable)."

Word by word:
- ขอ (kɔ̌ɔ) = would like to / may I
- ถวาย (tà-wǎai) = offer (to a monk)
- อาหาร (aa-hǎan) = food
- นี้ (níi) = this

Note: ถวาย is the special word for giving to a monk. Never use ให้ (hâi) here. I dropped "I" the way Thai speakers do, and kept ครับ/ค่ะ because everything said to a monk keeps the polite particle. Want a fuller version that names the monk by title?
