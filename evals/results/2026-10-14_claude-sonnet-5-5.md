### `translate-question-word`

**Q:** Translate: Are you hungry?

**หิวมั๊ยคะ/ครับ** (hǐu mái ká/kráp) = "Are you hungry?"

Note: Thai drops "you" here, and the question particle becomes ค๊ะ/ครับ in a question (ká for women, kráp for men). Books write ไหม, but people say มั๊ย (mái).

This is the Neutral rung, safe with anyone. Want a version for a friend (หิวมั๊ย, hǐu mái) or a monk?
