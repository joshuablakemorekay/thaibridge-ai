# v1 — the question as first asked

> How can we adapt the app to appeal to people who want Buddhism as universal.
> Buddhism taught like pure dhamma without the association of any culture or
> society? So that anyone in any part of the world feels they can learn it and
> adapt what they learn as to practice it wherever they are in the world; in
> whatever culture or society they exist? Is it possible to adapt the app like
> this while also preserving the existing Thai cultural side of it?

**What it produced.** A sound structural proposal — separate the universal
Dhamma from its Thai expression, keep both, explain the relationship.

**What it missed.** It went straight to proposing changes without checking what
the app already did. The finding that mattered most — that every Buddhism page
was *already* free and ungated, and only the copy said otherwise — did not
surface until the prompt was told to read the gate configuration first.
