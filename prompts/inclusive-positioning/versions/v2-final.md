# v2 — with the audit step and the two constraints

Three additions, each caused by a specific failure in v1 or its output.

**1. Audit before proposing.**

> Before proposing anything, check what the app's access rules actually grant.
> Read the gate configuration, not the marketing copy, and tell me where the two
> disagree.

This is what turned a copy rewrite into a finding.

**2. No country at all.**

> Universal must mean no country named at all — not my country substituted for
> Thailand.

Added after the draft proposed "practising the precepts in Britain".

**3. Describe the activity, not the identity.**

> Describe people by what they are learning, never by what they are not.

Added after "foreigner" was renamed to "non-Thai" — politer, structurally
identical. This constraint produces "for monks learning Thai" instead of "for
Western monks", and "you" instead of any label at all.
