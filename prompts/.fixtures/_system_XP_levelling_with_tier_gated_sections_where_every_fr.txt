The XP system is effective for two of your three audiences and decorative for
the third.

Free learners. Every free-tier section is level 1: home, alphabet, theravada,
meditation, chanting and paiboon. Legitimate play earns roughly 360 XP — 200
from the alphabet (100 unlock, 100 completion), 130 from the Dhamma sections,
and 30 in first-visit bonuses. That reaches Level 3 on the XP_LEVELS curve. It
unlocks nothing, because there is no level-2 or level-3 section that is not also
behind a paid tier. A free learner watches a progress bar fill toward a reward
that does not exist.

Monk Mode. The tier gate is waived, so XP is the only thing between a monastic
and the whole site. Here the levelling is real, earned progression.

Paying subscribers. Also real: the level requirements pace the content, so a
Basic subscriber cannot jump straight to Level 5 Sentences.

Three things are broken. max_level_access is set on all three tiers (5 / 7 / 10)
and is referenced nowhere else in the codebase — the docs advertise "Levels 6-7"
for Basic and nothing enforces it. Four of six POINT_REWARDS are dead: only
quiz_correct and first_visit are used, while quiz_perfect, daily_login,
complete_lesson and unlock_section are defined and never read. And
/api/award_points takes the XP amount from the client, so one POST can reach
level 10; nothing in the app calls it.
