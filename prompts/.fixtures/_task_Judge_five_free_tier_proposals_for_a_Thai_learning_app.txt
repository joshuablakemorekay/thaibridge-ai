I read SECTION_REQUIREMENTS and SUBSCRIPTION_TIERS before answering. Here is a
verdict on each proposal rather than a survey.

1. Complete Thai reading system free — DON'T. The sections vowels_syllables,
read_write and tones_classes ARE the Basic tier; the tier is literally named
"Thai Reader". Freeing them leaves £9.99 buying grammar, lessons and sentences
with no headline left. You already have a softer version of this generosity:
Level 3 earns one free Basic section, so a learner can choose reading and get it
free without the tier collapsing. The trade-off you would be accepting is the
loss of your paid tier's identity.

2. Paiboon lookup tool — DO. Search the romanisation the app already ships,
rather than transliterating. There is no transliterator in the codebase and
there should not be one: a live AI call costs money per lookup and can be subtly
wrong, and the person reaching for a lookup is a beginner who cannot tell a
hallucinated tone mark from a correct one. A miss must read as a miss.

3. One survival module — DO, and it is the strongest of the five. The free tier
is Buddhism plus an alphabet chart, so someone who arrives wanting to speak Thai
can finish nothing. Note that Tour Guide covers similar ground but is basic tier,
level 4 and alphabet-gated.

4. Reduce the 15 AI messages — DON'T. The costing is already in the code at
0.285p worst case per message, so 15 a day is about £1.30 a month for a free
user who maxes it out every single day, which almost nobody does. The tutor is
what makes £19.99 believable; shrinking your best free feature to save pennies
is the wrong trade.

5. Small free Dhamma allowance — DO, but it is a bug, not a feature. Tutor and
Dhamma share one cap, so a learner who spends the day on vocabulary is then
refused a question about the precepts. The comment above FREE_AI_DAILY_LIMIT
already states that charging for the follow-up question would be selling the
Dhamma. The gate does not honour that. Give Dhamma its own small pool, added on
top rather than reserved out of the existing 15.

6. Public curriculum outline — DO, and it is the cheapest of the lot. Generate
it from SECTION_REQUIREMENTS rather than hand-writing it, so it cannot go stale,
and show every section including the locked ones.
