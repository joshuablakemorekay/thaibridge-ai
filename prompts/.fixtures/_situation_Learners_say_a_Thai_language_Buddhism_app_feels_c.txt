Before proposing anything I read the gate configuration rather than the copy.

SECTION_REQUIREMENTS already grants what the learner is asking for: theravada,
meditation, chanting and paiboon are each level 1, tier free, with
requires_alphabet set to False. The comment beside the chanting entry even says
you do not need to read Thai to chant along. So the access rules and the page
copy disagree, and the copy is the part that is wrong. The home page says the
alphabet quiz will "unlock the site"; it unlocks the language track and nothing
in the Buddhism section.

That makes this a positioning problem rather than a permissions one, and it
means the fix does not require removing anything.

Keep the Thai cultural material. It is what makes the project distinctive, and
the objection was never to its presence — it was to its apparent compulsoriness.
Instead, separate the two things the app teaches and let a reader take either.

The universal layer is the Dhamma itself: the four noble truths, the eightfold
path, the precepts, metta and meditation. None of it depends on where a person
was born. The cultural layer is one tradition's expression of that teaching:
temple etiquette, ceremonies, the chanting tradition, and the vocabulary Thai has
absorbed from Pali. Present them side by side, equal in weight, with the
relationship explained, so a reader can see that the customs are examples rather
than requirements.

Practically: give the home page a way in that is not the alphabet, state on the
Dhamma pages that they need no Thai, and say on the culture pages that these
customs are one tradition's way of doing things.

For the page teaching lay practice, build it from frameworks that are universal
by construction rather than from worked examples, since an example always
carries a setting with it. The six directions of the Sigalovada Sutta work well:
every society has parents, teachers, partners, friends, people who work and
people who teach the teaching.
