I read the gate rather than the comment, and they disagree.

The check in /api/ai/chat compares usage['count'] against FREE_AI_DAILY_LIMIT
without looking at `mode` at all. So the tutor and the Dhamma both drain the
same 15 messages a day. A learner who spends fifteen messages drilling
vocabulary and then asks about the Five Precepts is told to come back tomorrow
or upgrade to Pro.

That is the contradiction. The comment above FREE_AI_DAILY_LIMIT says the
teaching is free and that charging for the follow-up question would be selling
the Dhamma. The teaching was never behind the paywall — but the question about
it now sits behind a language-study budget, which is the same line reached by a
side door. This is a bug, not a feature request.

The fix is to give the Dhamma its own counter. Two ways to do it, and the
difference matters:

- Reserve 5 of the existing 15. Costs nothing extra, but it is a takeaway:
  everyone's Thai tutor drops from 15 to 10 in order to fund a promise the site
  had already made.
- Add a separate 5 on top. Nobody loses anything. Using the 0.285p per message
  already costed in the file, that is about 1.4p a day for someone who exhausts
  it every single day, which almost nobody will.

Take the second.

The wording needs the same treatment as the gate. Running out of Dhamma
questions must not offer an upgrade at all — an upsell there is the site
charging for the teaching in all but name. And running out of TUTOR messages
should name the Dhamma questions still in hand before it mentions Pro, or the
nudge reads as a trick. The running total under the chat box has to follow the
mode too, or switching to Dhamma looks like it spends the tutor's allowance.
