Welcome to ThaiBridge AI, where the Buddhist teachings are free and open to everyone — no Thai needed. Thai heritage is honoured, respected and preserved here; not required.

Study the Dhamma as the Theravada tradition preserves it and Thai language, temples and traditions express it — or study and practise it without adopting any particular cultural identity or tradition at all.

The Dhamma is Universal. How you understand it and use it in your own life is yours to decide, whatever culture you are part of. Keep the Dhamma close to your heart yet remain detached.
