The Buddhist teachings here are free and open to everyone — no Thai needed. Thai heritage is honoured, respected and preserved here; it is not required.

You are free to explore how the Buddha's Dhamma is preserved in the Theravada tradition as expressed through Thai language, society, temples and traditions, or to study and practise the Dhamma without adopting any particular cultural identity or tradition at all.

How the teachings are understood and applied in your own life remains yours to determine, whatever culture you are a part of. The Dhamma is universal. Learn Thai, discover the culture that has carried these teachings for centuries, and choose your own path through them on your own terms.
