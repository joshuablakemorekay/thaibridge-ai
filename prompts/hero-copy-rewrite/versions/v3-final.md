# v3 — final, author-written

The shipped copy is the author's own text, not an AI draft. It restored the
branch v2 had lost and borrowed `/dhamma-and-culture`'s own language so the two
pages agree rather than paraphrase each other.

Live on the home page (commit `9cf3302`):

> 🪷 The Buddhist teachings here are free and open to everyone — no Thai
> needed. In this app, Thai heritage is honoured, respected and preserved; not
> required. You are free to explore and understand how the Buddha's Dhamma is
> preserved in the Theravada tradition as expressed through Thai language,
> society, temples and traditions, or to study and practise the Dhamma without
> adopting any particular cultural identity or tradition at all. You the
> Learner remain free to determine how the teachings can be understood and
> applied in your own life, to make it better and happier, whatever culture you
> are a part of. The Dhamma is Universal! Keep the Dhamma close to your heart
> yet remain detached. Learn Thai. Discover the culture that connects them — on
> your own terms, with the freedom to choose your own path.

**What changed from v2, and why it is better:** both branches are now named —
through the tradition, *or* without adopting any cultural identity at all — and
the host culture leads as something honoured and preserved rather than as the
thing being declined.

**Reusable lesson:** the constraints in the v3 prompt were all discovered by
rejecting drafts. Stating them up front is what makes the prompt worth keeping.
