# v1 — the opening brief

> "This doesn't sound very convincing."

Aimed at the hero as it then stood:

```
🪷 ThaiBridge AI
Theravada Buddhism & the Thai language · พระธรรมและภาษาไทย
Two subjects, side by side. Study the Dhamma on its own, learn Thai on its
own, or follow how the two meet in Thailand's temples.
```

**What this version got right:** nothing was specified, and that turned out to
be enough — the fault was diagnosable from the copy alone (it describes the
site's structure, and its hedges read as an apology for two subjects sharing a
page).

**What it could not do:** with no constraints attached, the rewrite that came
back optimised for clarity and said nothing about culture. Every constraint in
v3 was discovered by rejecting a draft, not stated up front.
